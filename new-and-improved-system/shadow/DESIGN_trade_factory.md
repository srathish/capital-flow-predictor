# Trade-rule factory — ~500 intraday entry/exit rules on SPY / QQQ / IWM / DIA (locked 2026-10-09, before any engine)

**Question each rule answers:** trading this exact entry and exit, after costs, is the average trade positive, does it
hold on unseen days, and does it beat the SAME rule run on random levels (so the level itself matters)?
Registry: `trade_registry.json` (generated by `trade_registry.mjs`, committed with this file before any results).

## Data
- UW 1-minute bars with pre-market (`.cache/uw/spy1m/<d>.json`; QQQ / IWM / DIA pulled the same way into
  `.cache/uw/{qqq,iwm,dia}1m/`), 2023-01-03 → 2026-10-02. Regular session 09:30–16:00 ET.
- UW daily greek exposure by strike for the 4 ETFs (`.cache/uwgreeks/{T}.jsonl`, priced at each day's close, from
  2023-11-08) for the gamma levels and the regime filter; UW daily OHLC for prior-day levels.
- Everything a rule uses on day d is known before the bar it acts on: prior-day levels and gamma from d−1's close,
  premarket levels from bars before 09:30, opening ranges only after they complete, VWAP from bars up to that minute.

## Rule = level × trigger × exit × filter
**Levels (16):** prior-day high / low; premarket high / low; opening-range high / low for 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes;
session VWAP; prior-day gamma king (strike with the largest |net gamma| on d−1); call wall (largest call gamma strike
above the d−1 close); put wall (largest |put gamma| strike below it). (OR15 / OR60 are used only with the breakout
triggers.)
**Triggers (4):**
- `BRK` breakout: a 5-minute bar closes beyond the level, coming from the other side → enter at that close in the
  direction of the break. Stop: 0.10% back through the level.
- `RET` break-and-retest: after a BRK-type close beyond the level, price returns to touch it within 60 minutes and that
  5-minute bar closes on the breakout side → enter at that close. Stop: 0.10% back through the level.
- `RCL` sweep-and-reclaim ("Timmy hunt", raked's entry B, as in DESIGN_timmy.md): touch, trade ≥ 0.01% through, a
  3-minute close back on the original side within 15 minutes → enter at that close, reversal direction. Stop: beyond the
  sweep extreme by 0.0033% and ≥ 0.0165% past the level; skip if risk > 0.165%.
- `FAD` first-touch fade: limit order at the level on its first touch of the day from one side, reversal direction.
  Stop: 0.10% through the level.
**Exits (3):** `T2` fixed 2R target; `NXT` the next level of the day (any of the 16) beyond the entry in the trade
direction, 0.0033% before it (skip if < 1R away); `EOD` no target, exit at 15:55. Every exit also has the stop; if a bar
touches stop and target, the stop counts; a gap through the stop fills at the bar's open.
**Filters (3):** `ALL` none; `GAM` the validated gamma regime (idea factory G23: local ±2% gamma balance z-scored vs its
own prior 60 days, from d−1's close) — breakout triggers (BRK, RET) only on days with z below the build-period median
(less local long gamma → wider day), reversal triggers (RCL, FAD) only on days above it; `VW` trade only on the VWAP side
of the direction (long above VWAP, short below) at entry.

**Grid:** 12 core levels × 4 triggers × 3 exits × 3 filters = 432, plus OR15 / OR60 high / low × {BRK, RET} × 3 exits ×
3 filters = 72 → **504 rules.** Gamma-level and GAM rules start 2023-11-09 (first day with d−1 greeks).
**Per rule:** at most one trade per symbol per day (the first signal), entries 09:35–15:30. Cost: $0.01 per share per
side. R = P&L ÷ (entry − stop distance). Trades pooled across the 4 symbols.

## Statistics and validation
- Mean R per trade with a day-clustered t (all symbols' trades on a day form one cluster), one-sided (rules are
  registered as "mean R > 0").
- **Build:** 2023-01-03 → 2025-03-31 (gamma rules 2023-11-09 →). **Holdout:** 2025-04-01 → 2026-10-02.
- Stage 1: Benjamini–Hochberg q = 0.10 across all 504 on the build. Stage 2: BH q = 0.10 across survivors on the holdout.
- **Own placebo (random-level twins):** each validated rule is re-run 20 times on the holdout with every level moved by
  its own random offset of ±0.15–0.60% (fixed seeds; dynamic levels like VWAP shifted by the same fraction all day). A
  rule is **validated** only if it passes stage 2 AND its holdout mean R beats ≥ 19 of its 20 twins.
- Reported for validated rules: trades, win rate, mean R, holdout per symbol, per year, and after a $0.02/side cost.

## Amendments
**Amendment 1 (2026-10-10, after the independent audit, before the run).**
- Missing regular-session minutes (DIA: 677 of 933 days, mostly 2024-02 → 2025-07) are filled with flat bars at the
  previous close (volume 0) so the 5-minute grid and look-backs stay aligned.
- Isolated bad-print wicks are clipped to 0.2% beyond the bar's own and neighbours' open/close (they were setting fake
  prior-day / premarket highs and lows); premarket levels use bars with volume only.
- No entries before 09:35 for FAD and RCL. Intrabar triggers (FAD, RCL) and their VWAP filter read VWAP through the
  previous bar. FAD signals whose fill is already beyond the stop are skipped. RCL re-arm is checked before any skip.
- VW = "the first signal that is on the VWAP side of the trade". The 9 VWAP × VW rules are dropped (BRK/RET identical to
  ALL by construction; FAD impossible): **495 rules**.
- RET = any breakout of the level that day (not only the first) with a retest within 60 minutes; a close back through
  between break and retest does not cancel it. NXT with no valid target skips that rule's day.
- Half days are kept (EOD exit at their last bar). GAM's z-score needs 40 prior days, so GAM rules start 2024-01-09.
- Twins keep the real VWAP for the VW filter; a twin with no trades counts as not beaten.
