# Stock-finding factory — ~500 studies of which stocks outperform (locked 2026-10-09, before any engine run)

**Question each study answers:** does one feature, known at the rebalance date, rank stocks by what they do next?
Registry: `stock_registry.json` (generated by `stock_registry.mjs`, committed with this file before any results).

## Two horizons
| | LONG (buy-and-hold style) | SHORT (options-swing style) |
|---|---|---|
| Stocks | SEC operating companies in `universeV2`, eligible at each date: price ≥ $5, 50-day $ volume ≥ $20M, not a fund, clean price layer (no quarantine window) | the 300 most liquid stocks **as of 2023-10-31** (`.cache/uwgreeks_w/tickers.json`), chosen before the study period |
| Rebalance | month-ends | last trading day of each week |
| Data | SEC XBRL first-filed values (filed ≤ date), prices, insider purchases (filed ≤ date), guidance 8-Ks (filed ≤ date), filing concepts (10-K/10-Q dates ≤ date), commodity ETFs | UW greek exposure by strike at that close (split factor detected per snapshot), prices, SEC fundamentals |
| Outcomes | M1, M3, M6 = 1/3/6-month return vs the eligible-universe average; MV = top 10% of 6-month returns (1/0) | W1, W2, W4 = 1/2/4-week return vs the 300 average; WV = top 10% of 4-week returns (1/0) |
| Entry / exit | close of the first trading day after the rebalance date → close on the last trading day of the horizon | same, weekly |
| Build | 2012-01 → 2019-12 (forward windows must end by 2019-12-31) | 2023-11 → 2025-06 (windows end by 2025-06-30) |
| Holdout | 2020-01 → 2026-03 | 2025-07 → 2026-09 |

**Split-adjusted market caps.** Prices are adjusted to today's share basis, SEC share counts are as-reported. Market cap
uses `split_shares.mjs`: a split is a share-count jump by a standard ratio ≥ 2 that is **confirmed by SEC restating an
earlier period** (shares ×ratio or EPS ÷ratio; dilution never restates). Verified on NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, GOOGL, AMZN,
AVGO, WMT, CMG, SMCI, LRCX, DECK, NFLX, ANET, GE. (The adopted v6 P/S cap used raw shares × adjusted prices — a
look-ahead bias toward later splitters; it is re-tested here as L26/L28.)

## Statistic
For each study and each rebalance date: Spearman rank correlation (IC) between the feature and the outcome across the
eligible stocks (dates with < 50 stocks with the feature are skipped). The study's effect = mean IC; t = mean IC ÷
Newey–West s.e. with lag = the outcome's length in rebalance periods (M1 1, M3 3, M6/MV 6; W1 1, W2 2, W4/WV 4).
Plain-units effect reported for validated studies: average outcome of the top fifth minus bottom fifth by the feature.

## Validation (same as the idea factory)
1. **Build:** one-sided p in the predicted direction (`?` = two-sided); Benjamini–Hochberg q = 0.10 across all studies
   of that horizon.
2. **Holdout:** each survivor re-estimated once; BH q = 0.10 across the survivors on one-sided p in the build direction.
3. **Own placebo:** the real holdout t must beat ≥ 19 of 20 holdout fits of the same study in which every stock is given
   the feature series of another stock (a fixed random permutation of tickers per placebo, so persistence and
   cross-sectional spread are kept but the link to each stock's own future is cut).
4. Reported for validated studies: industry-neutral IC (feature ranked within SIC 2-digit), IC in small / large halves by
   market cap, and whether it adds beyond the strongest validated study of the same outcome (two-feature
   cross-sectional regression on ranks, Newey–West t).

## Known limits
Survivorship (the SEC list is today's companies; delisted names are missing) — returns are biased up, rank
correlations less so. Several features were used in earlier designs (`seen` in the registry); they are reported but
flagged. The short horizon has only ~20 months of build and ~15 months of holdout.

## Run discipline
Engines `stock_factory_long.mjs` and `stock_factory_short.mjs` audited independently before their single runs; fixes
logged below. Output `shadow/results_stock_factory/`. Research only — nothing live changes.

## Amendments
**Amendment 1 (2026-10-09, after the independent audit of the long engine, before its run).**
- **Eligibility (long):** market cap ≥ $500M at M (split-adjusted SEC shares filed ≤ M × price) replaces price ≥ $5 and
  $20M dollar volume. Those filters ran on split-adjusted prices and excluded future splitters (NVDA, AVGO, TSLA, NFLX …)
  in early years — a look-ahead bias against later winners. (The same filter is in v5 / engines / paper_money; reported.)
- **Fundamentals:** per period the EARLIEST-filed value across tags is kept (tag order only breaks ties). Before, ASC 606
  restated comparatives filed in 2018 replaced original 2017 values, making 36% of 2017 quarters visible a year late.
  (Also present in v5 / engines; reported.)
- Insider and guidance features (L52–L56) are missing, not zero, before 2014-03 (their files start 2014).
- Concept features (L48–L50) only for stocks with a concept mention filed ≤ M; concept returns exclude the stock itself;
  L50 needs a mention history older than a year. Coverage note: 2014–2021 concept file covers ~3,200 tickers, the 2022+
  file ~380, so holdout L48–L50 run on far fewer stocks. Guidance events roughly double from 2021.
- **L57 (spin-off) dropped** — a spin-off cannot meet the 252-trading-day history rule within its first year. 500 studies.
- Months with no spread in a feature (all stocks equal) are skipped instead of voiding the study. L59/L62 are missing when
  an input is missing; L61 = 1 if any matching commodity group is in an uptrend.
- Holdout uses every month whose forward window is complete (M1 to 2026-08, M3 to 2026-06, M6/MV to 2026-03).
- Known limits: prices are also dividend-adjusted, so past market caps of later dividend payers are understated by
  their later dividends (valuation and size features carry a small look-ahead); SIC codes are today's.

**Amendment 2 (2026-10-09, after the independent audit of the short engine, before its run).**
- **Universe rebuilt** (`weekly_universe.mjs`): 300 highest 50-day RAW dollar volume as of 2023-10-31 (raw price =
  adjusted × later split factor; volume on its raw basis — UW volume is unadjusted for some tickers, e.g. AVGO, which the
  first list wrongly excluded); commodity/fund trusts (SIC 6221/6722/6726: SLV, USO, IAU) excluded. Known limit: the SEC
  list is today's companies, so 2023 names later acquired/delisted (PXD, SPLK, HES, DFS, ANSS, JNPR, MRO, X) are missing.
- **Split basis** (`weekly_basis.mjs`): UW daily bars are split-adjusted for some tickers and not others (KLAC, ODFL,
  CELH, MNST, NVO, AZN, AMC). A price break is a split/corporate action only if the option-chain centre moved with the
  price (strikes follow a split; a crash leaves them behind) or it matches an SEC-confirmed split; such breaks are
  back-adjusted. Week factors come only from confirmed events (the collector's 1.5 snaps after crashes — INTC, MU, RIVN,
  KVUE, SMCI, PYPL, SHOP — are gone; BKNG's 25:1 is handled). Snapshots within 7 days of a break, or ambiguous inside an
  SEC filing bracket, are unused. Rows whose 252-day lookback crosses an unexplained break (e.g. AMC 2023-08) are dropped.
- The collector keeps strikes within ±30% of every candidate raw price and re-fetches snapshots stored with a wrong
  window; XYZ before 2025-01-21 is fetched as SQ.
- UW exposures are per share: S15 and S53–S56 now use dollar units (delta, vanna × price; gamma × price² × 0.01).
- AVGO-type raw volume is put on the split-adjusted basis. **S43 dropped** (identical ranks to S37): 220 short studies.
- Noted: outcomes are price-only (no dividends); GOLD (Barrick → B, 2025) has a price gap.
