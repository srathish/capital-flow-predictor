## Recurring rules

**Only trade clean maps — messy, divergent, shuffling or unreadable maps mean no trade** (Glitch, Giul, jack, Hamoudi — 119) — Map quality is itself the filter: clear nodes, air pockets, aligned structure. Messy, split, frozen, stale or heavily divergent maps = cash. Flat is a position; Heatseeker's highest value is telling you whether it's a good day to trade at all.
- *"Map all day said don't trade. If you traded and are green, good job, if you traded and are red, lesson learned."* (Glitch, 2025-08-01)

**Never chase — wait for the setup to come to you** (Glitch, Giul, jack, TheArchitect, Hamoudi — 89) — Buying extended moves, pumps, tops, gap fills or the middle of a range inherits someone else's entry and burns premium. If you missed the level, wait for the retest or skip it.
- *"Chasers always get burnt, pigs get slaughtered, market will reward the patient"* (Glitch, 2025-08-05)
- *"PLEASE for the love of god, stop chasing."* (Glitch, 2026-05-19)

**Chop / pin / whipsaw / trap regime: name it, then stand down** (Glitch, Hamoudi, jack, Giul, TheArchitect — 76) — Ranges, vice grips, pin jobs and sandwiches are dealer-engineered max-pain structures where apparent breaks are fake. Recognize the regime before picking a strategy, and when it's chop, log off.

- *"It's a trap day lol / Anytime u think we finally broke ceiling we prob didn't"* (Hamoudi, 2026-09-30)

**Take profits — trim, lock in, trail; never let green go red** (Glitch, Giul, jack, TheArchitect, Hamoudi — 73) — Sell into strength before the candle confirms, trim at ~20%, move stops to breakeven, make it risk-free. Nobody will tell you when to sell.

## Rules that are about NOT trading

Ranked by total count — the no-trade cluster is the single heaviest body of guidance in the corpus.

1. **Messy / divergent / shuffling / unreadable map = cash** (119) — no edge, no trade; Heatseeker's top value is telling you when *not* to trade.
2. **Chop / pin / whipsaw / trap regime = stand down** (76) — name the regime first; pinned tape has no trade in it.
3. **Never force a trade; stop once you're paid or after one loss** (55) — flat is a valid outcome, there's always tomorrow, no revenge trading.
4. **Don't trade the event** (57) — FOMC, CPI, OpEx, EOM/EOQ, earnings, data prints, pressers, MOC.
5. **No trigger / no confirmation / level failed = no entry** (56 + 11) — the bias dies at the open if it gaps through; an unconfirmed level is a hypothesis.
6. **Capital preservation: raise cash, hedge, stay small** (32) — cash is a position.
7. **Avoid earnings entirely** (26) — a coin flip you can't model; IV crush plus theta.
8. **No support node underneath = no long; no node to target = no trade** (19) — no defined risk, no magnet, no position.
9. **No clear direction / insufficient R:R = no position** (11) — ping-pong ranges, $4-move targets, declining average setup move.
10. **Avoid dead hours** (33) — the first 15-45 min before the shuffle settles, lunch, and the last minutes where maps flip repeatedly.
11. **Never short the hole / don't fade a reverse rug or barney ceiling** (28) — structurally capped side; also don't re-short a delivered node.
12. **Downside node accumulation = don't be long** (40) — not a dip buy; don't hold it overnight.
13. **Index/market regime veto** (12) — a market dump or lost SMH/QQQ level invalidates every single-name long beneath it.
14. **Don't hold risk into weekends** (12).
15. **OPEX price action is untradeable fakery** (8).
16. **Never play breakout/breakdown retests on lower timeframes** (11) — a false break is indistinguishable from a retest.
17. **Pika stacks / thick walls / tight ranges cap both sides** (21) — nothing to play through.
18. **Illiquid chains, wide spreads, bad bid/ask, blacklisted tickers = pass** (11) — disqualifies an otherwise fine setup.
19. **Don't take the lotto if you're red** (9) — lottos are funded from the day's profit.
