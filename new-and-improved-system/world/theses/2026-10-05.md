# Thesis engine — 2026-10-05

## Memory is the new GPU: AI inference shifts the bottleneck from logic to HBM/DRAM/NAND bits (confidence 0.7)
- **Chain:** AI capex has over-provisioned GPU compute but each inference token consumes memory bandwidth and capacity, not just FLOPs → HBM attention jumped +72% across filers while its basket is up only +2% — companies are talking about it, the market has not re-rated the owners (A) → NAND scarcity language exploded +884% and DRAM +392% off a near-zero base, and the voices are DEMAND-side buyers (AAPL, LI, MU) flagging they cannot get bits (B) → Memory fabs are capex- and yield-constrained; HBM steals wafer capacity from commodity DRAM/NAND, so a demand shock tightens the WHOLE memory complex, not one node → Owners of the constrained bit supply (MU, SNDK, WDC) see ASPs and margins inflect while the tape still prices them as late-cycle commodity cyclicals
- **Bottleneck:** HBM/DRAM/NAND bit supply and memory-fab wafer capacity
- **Beneficiaries:** MU, SNDK, WDC
- **Why now:** NAND/DRAM scarcity language is up ~400-900% off zero (B) and HBM attention +72% with basket +2% (A) — the attention/price gap is widest here of any concept
- **Priced in:** Almost nothing: HBM basket +2%, NAND/DRAM scarcity baskets ~-1% (A,B). Smart money disagrees with the tape — SA LP holds 56% in SNDK+MU (D)
- **Wrong if:** DRAM/NAND spot contract prices roll over 2 quarters in a row, HBM qualification orders from hyperscalers get pushed, or memory makers guide capex UP into a demand air-pocket (classic top). Any of these within 6 months kills it.

## Power and the electrical interconnect, not silicon, is the hard physical ceiling on the AI buildout (confidence 0.6)
- **Chain:** A data center is useless without megawatts delivered to the rack; the US grid, transformers, and switchgear have decade-long lead times → grid scarcity language +78% and data-center scarcity +25% (B), yet the grid basket is -13% and data-center basket -5% (A) — the constraint is loud but unpriced → The named scarcity voices are the builders themselves (CORZ, IREN, GDS, AAON on data center; CLF, FE, NRG on grid) (B) — supply-side capitulation, not hype → Electrical-equipment and power-gen owners (GEV, ETN, VRT, CEG, VST) sell into multi-year backlogs with pricing power as utilities and hyperscalers bid for scarce capacity → Earnings must rise because backlog coverage and book-to-bill expand while the component (transformers, cooling, firm power) stays sold out
- **Bottleneck:** Grid interconnect capacity, high-voltage electrical gear, and firm 24/7 power
- **Beneficiaries:** GEV, ETN, VRT, CEG, VST
- **Why now:** Grid scarcity +78% and data-center scarcity +25% are among the fastest-rising shortage signals (B) while the baskets are deeply negative (A)
- **Priced in:** Lightly: grid basket -13%, data-center -5% (A). Note 'transformer' basket is -12% (A) — that sub-theme already ran and corrected, so I lean to grid/electrical breadth not transformers specifically
- **Wrong if:** Hyperscaler capex guides flat/down for 2026, data-center interconnect queues start clearing, or GEV/ETN/VRT book-to-bill drops below 1.0 within 6 months.

## HBM and advanced packaging are etch-and-deposition intensive — the pick-and-shovel layer under the memory shock (confidence 0.6)
- **Chain:** HBM stacks and advanced packaging (hybrid bonding, TSV) require far more etch/deposition steps per wafer than planar logic → etch attention +51% and deposition +3% with baskets roughly flat (-2%/-1%) (A) — rising mention, no price → If Thesis 1 (memory shortage) is right, memory-makers must add capacity, and capacity = WFE orders to the etch/deposition/inspection leaders → Owners (LRCX, AMAT, KLAC, ONTO, ACMR) capture the capex regardless of which memory maker wins the HBM share war — a lower-variance way to own the memory bottleneck → Their earnings rise as memory WFE re-accelerates off a trough while the market still models flat 2026 WFE
- **Bottleneck:** Advanced etch/deposition/inspection tool capacity for HBM and packaging
- **Beneficiaries:** LRCX, AMAT, KLAC, ONTO, ACMR
- **Why now:** etch attention +51% (A) directly precedes the memory-capacity response implied by the +884% NAND scarcity signal (B)
- **Priced in:** Modestly: etch/deposition baskets ~flat (A). GPU basket +4% shows the logic-WFE leg is more discovered than the memory-WFE leg
- **Wrong if:** Memory makers cut or delay capex, LRCX/AMAT memory-segment revenue mix shrinks for 2 quarters, or China WFE restrictions deepen enough to offset HBM demand — within 6 months.

## Foundry and EUV are the un-substitutable chokepoint — own the toll-booth (confidence 0.65)
- **Chain:** Every leading-edge AI chip and HBM base-die routes through TSMC; CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity is the single tightest line in the AI supply chain → EUV lithography has one supplier (ASML); there is no second source at the node → Inference attention +26% and data-center breadth across 284 cos (A) means volume, not just price, flows through the one foundry and the one litho vendor → Earnings must rise because TSMC prices CoWoS/leading-edge up into scarcity and ASML's backlog is multi-year and order-covered → SA LP holds TSM at 6% (D) — the smart-money anchor agrees this is the durable toll
- **Bottleneck:** Leading-edge foundry + CoWoS packaging (TSM) and EUV scanners (ASML)
- **Beneficiaries:** TSM, ASML
- **Why now:** This is the lower-beta anchor to the memory/power theses; inference +26% broadens the demand base (A)
- **Priced in:** Partially — these are consensus AI winners, so sized smaller than the unpriced memory/grid legs. Edge is durability, not surprise.
- **Wrong if:** TSMC CoWoS capacity additions outrun demand (utilization dips), ASML bookings miss for 2 quarters, or a credible leading-edge second source emerges within 6 months.

## Copper is the embedded commodity in every megawatt of AI power (confidence 0.45)
- **Chain:** Electrification of data centers (Thesis 2) is copper-intensive: busbars, transformers, cabling, cooling → copper attention +13% with basket only -4% (A) — rising mention, price still soft → Mine supply is inelastic (decade-long permitting/ramp) while AI-power demand is a step-change on top of EV/grid baseline → Owners of low-cost copper reserves (FCX, SCCO) see realized prices rise against a fixed cost base, expanding margins → This is the second-derivative of the power thesis and the least-crowded expression of it
- **Bottleneck:** Mined copper supply vs. electrification demand
- **Beneficiaries:** FCX, SCCO
- **Why now:** copper attention +13% while grid/data-center scarcity surges (A,B) — demand signal building, price not yet there
- **Priced in:** Barely: copper basket -4% (A). Lowest-confidence leg because it's macro/China-sensitive, so sized small.
- **Wrong if:** China property/demand weakens further, copper inventories build, or a global-growth scare drops the price under cost-curve support within 6 months.
