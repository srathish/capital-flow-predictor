# Data fix: clean price layer (2026-10-06)

Found while checking the engines: UW daily history contains
1. bars on NYSE holidays with unadjusted prices (EXLS ×5 on MLK Day 2010, Good Friday 2013 — a month-end — July 4 2013,
   Thanksgiving 2014), which snap back the next day; SPY's UW history has them too, so a UW-derived calendar does not catch them;
2. stretches where a split was not adjusted (EXLS ×5 from 2022-07-05 until its 2023-08 5:1 split).

2,775 one-day moves beyond −70%/+230% across 679 tickers (some real, many errors).

**Fix** (`world/prices_clean.mjs`): a rule-based NYSE trading calendar (incl. Good Friday, Juneteenth from 2022, and the 2012
Sandy / 2018 Bush / 2025 Carter closures) drops non-trading-day bars; every persistent level jump at a clean split ratio (×2…×100
or the inverse, not reverting within 3 days) opens a quarantine window (200 days before → 400 after) in which the ticker is not
eligible and held positions are exited before it. Nothing is repaired. 597 tickers get ≥ 1 window (mostly sub-$5 names).

**Re-runs:** every verdict-producing script now reads the clean layer. The pre-fix results are kept in
`world/results_preclean/`. The same locked rules are re-run on the corrected data and both versions are reported.
